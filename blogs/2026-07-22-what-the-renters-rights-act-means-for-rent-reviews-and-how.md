The Renters’ Rights Act abolished Section 21 on 1st May 2026. Every rent increase now has to go through a formal Section 13 notice, and every tenant has the right to challenge it at tribunal. For letting agents , that turns rent reviews from an informal conversation into a process that needs to hold up under scrutiny.
